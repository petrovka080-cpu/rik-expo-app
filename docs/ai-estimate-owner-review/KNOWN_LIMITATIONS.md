## Pricebook And Contract Total

The estimate may show preliminary totals only. A contract total is forbidden until the
owner approves the business policy and all required price coverage and trusted sources
are accepted.

## High-Risk Work

The controlled pilot excludes structural design, gas work, high-voltage electrical work,
fire safety systems, medical or industrial facilities, and regulated public infrastructure.

## Evidence Boundaries

Optional layers that do not have current evidence must be marked not evaluated. They
must not be represented as green.

## User-Facing Constraints

Known limitations must stay visible in owner review, support playbooks, and the owner
review PDF packet.
