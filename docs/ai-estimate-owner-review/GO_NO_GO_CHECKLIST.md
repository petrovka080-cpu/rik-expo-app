- Pricebook limitations visible.
- Contract total not claimed.
- Approved history scaling green.
- PDF and buyer handoff green.
- Support playbook ready.
- Rollback plan ready.
- Kill switch ready.
- Owner decision pending until owner changes it.

The agent cannot auto-set GO. The owner must decide GO, NO-GO, or HOLD.
