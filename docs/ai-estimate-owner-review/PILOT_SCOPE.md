## Included

- Consumer repair estimates.
- Apartment renovation and finishing work estimates.
- Low-voltage electrical and plumbing repair estimates.
- Foreman material estimate review.
- Foreman subcontract estimate review.
- Director review, PDF generation, and buyer handoff evidence.

## Excluded

- Production release.
- Public beta.
- Native build, EAS, TestFlight, App Store, or Play Store activity.
- Production DB changes or destructive migrations.
- Marketplace, RFQ, warehouse, and payment flows.
- Contract total claims.

## Cohort

The pilot cohort is limited to 20 users until owner review changes the limit.
