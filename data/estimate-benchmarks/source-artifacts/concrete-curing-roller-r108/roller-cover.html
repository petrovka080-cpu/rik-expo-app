WhiteDove™

The Purdy® WhiteDove™ paint roller provides a smooth, lint-free finish. Made with premium woven fabric, this roller cover comes in a variety of naps to work best with your painting area. From semi-smooth to semi-rough surfaces, use this applicator with latex and oil-based paints, primers, stains and clears in any sheen. Ideal for drywall, ceilings and floors.

  • Provides the most lint free-finish
  • Premium woven fabric
  • 4", 4-1/2", 6-1/2", 7", 9", 12" 14" and 18" rollers available
  • Available in 1/4", 3/8", 1/2" and 3/4" naps
  • For semi-smooth to semi-rough surfaces
  • Use with latex and oil-based paints, primers, stains and clears
  • Great for all sheens
  • Ideal for drywall, ceilings and floors
